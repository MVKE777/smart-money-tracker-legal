Frequently Asked Questions

What does Smart Money Tracker do?

It helps you explore cryptocurrency market trends, whale activity, emerging tokens, token market data, AI-powered insights, and personal watchlists.

Why is some market information missing or delayed?

Market data comes from external data providers and blockchain sources. Availability and update frequency may vary by token or network. Try refreshing the app and checking again later.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple Account name, then choose Subscriptions. Select Smart Money Tracker to review or cancel your subscription. You can also manage subscriptions through the App Store.

What if my PRO features are not available after purchase?

Confirm that you're using the Apple Account associated with the purchase, then use the Restore Purchases option in the app if available. If your subscription still isn't recognized, contact support.

Does the app provide financial advice?

No. Smart Money Tracker provides information and research tools only. Nothing in the app is personalized financial or investment advice, and no market signal guarantees an outcome.

When contacting support, please describe the issue and include your app version and iOS version if possible. Never send passwords, wallet seed phrases, private keys, or payment card details.
