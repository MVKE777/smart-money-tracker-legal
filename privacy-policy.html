Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Smart Money Tracker respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

1. What the App Does

Smart Money Tracker provides crypto market signals, whale activity, token data, AI analysis, and watchlist features for informational purposes only.

The app does not provide financial advice. All market signals and AI-generated insights are for informational purposes only.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Smart Money Tracker does not ask for or collect:

  • wallet seed phrases
  • private keys
  • exchange login details
  • banking information
  • payment card details
  • personal trading account information
  • identity documents

3. Information Used by the App

The app may use public market and blockchain-related data, including token names, symbols, contract addresses, price movement, liquidity, trading volume, whale transactions, token security flags, and market activity signals.

If you use AI Analysis, selected token market data may be processed to generate an informational AI insight.

4. Watchlist

Tokens added to Watchlist may be stored locally on your device.

You can remove this local data by removing tokens from Watchlist or deleting the app.

5. Subscriptions and Purchases

PRO subscriptions are processed through the Apple App Store.

We do not directly collect or store your payment card details. Apple may provide subscription status information so the app can unlock PRO features.

6. Third-Party Services

Smart Money Tracker may use third-party services to provide market data, security checks, AI analysis, backend functionality, and subscriptions.

These services may include:

  • CoinGecko
  • DexScreener
  • GeckoTerminal
  • GoPlus
  • OpenAI
  • Supabase
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit
  • Adapty, if used for subscription management

These third-party services may process technical or usage-related data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect app-related data. However, no internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children’s Privacy

Smart Money Tracker is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
