Smart Money Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Smart Money Tracker, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

1. About Smart Money Tracker

Smart Money Tracker provides cryptocurrency market intelligence and research tools, including market data, whale activity, token information, AI-generated analysis, market signals, risk indicators, and watchlist features.

The App is provided for informational and research purposes only and does not provide financial, investment, trading, legal, or tax advice.

2. Information We Do Not Request

Smart Money Tracker does not directly request or require:

  • wallet seed phrases;
  • cryptocurrency private keys;
  • exchange login credentials;
  • banking credentials;
  • payment card numbers;
  • identity documents;
  • access to cryptocurrency wallets;
  • access to cryptocurrency exchange accounts.

Smart Money Tracker does not provide cryptocurrency custody, wallet, brokerage, exchange, or trade execution services.

3. Information Processed by the App

The App may process public cryptocurrency market and blockchain information, including:

  • token names and symbols;
  • contract addresses;
  • blockchain network information;
  • token prices and price changes;
  • market capitalization;
  • liquidity;
  • trading volume;
  • public blockchain transactions;
  • whale activity;
  • token security indicators;
  • market activity and sentiment signals.

This information is generally obtained from public blockchain networks and third-party market data providers.

4. AI Analysis

When you use AI-powered features, selected cryptocurrency market information may be sent to an AI service to generate informational analysis.

We do not intentionally send wallet seed phrases, private keys, banking credentials, payment card information, or other sensitive financial credentials to AI providers.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated and should not be treated as financial advice.

5. Watchlist and Local App Data

Information such as tokens added to your Watchlist and certain app preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Local data can generally be removed by:

  • removing items from the Watchlist;
  • clearing applicable app data where available; or
  • deleting the App from your device.

6. Subscriptions and Purchases

Smart Money Tracker may offer optional PRO subscription features.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store and Apple In-App Purchase system. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card details.

To provide and manage subscription access, Apple and our subscription management provider, Adapty, may process information such as:

  • subscription status;
  • purchased product identifiers;
  • purchase and renewal events;
  • transaction-related information provided by the App Store;
  • anonymous subscription profile identifiers;
  • device and app information required to operate subscription functionality.

This information is used to determine whether PRO features should be available to you, restore purchases, manage subscription status, and support subscription-related functionality.

7. Anonymous Subscription Profiles

Smart Money Tracker does not currently require users to create a Smart Money Tracker account.

When subscription management is enabled, Adapty may create an anonymous profile associated with the App installation in order to manage purchases and subscription access.

This profile does not require you to provide your name, email address, or Smart Money Tracker account credentials.

8. Third-Party Services

Smart Money Tracker relies on third-party services to provide certain functionality. Depending on the feature being used, these services may include:

  • CoinGecko;
  • GeckoTerminal;
  • DexScreener;
  • Blockchair;
  • mempool.space;
  • GoPlus;
  • AI service providers;
  • Supabase, where used for backend functionality;
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit;
  • Adapty for subscription management.

When the App communicates with these services, they may receive technical information necessary to respond to requests, such as network information, device or app information, request metadata, and the public cryptocurrency data being requested.

Each third-party provider processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

9. Technical Information

Third-party infrastructure and service providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to provide their services, such as:

  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • app version;
  • network request information;
  • diagnostic information;
  • subscription-related identifiers.

Where supported and not required for App functionality, we may configure service providers to reduce unnecessary collection of identifiers such as advertising identifiers or IP-derived information.

10. How We Use Information

Information processed through Smart Money Tracker may be used to:

  • provide cryptocurrency market information;
  • display public blockchain activity;
  • generate AI-powered analysis;
  • maintain Watchlist functionality;
  • provide and verify PRO subscription access;
  • restore purchases;
  • maintain and improve App reliability;
  • diagnose technical problems;
  • prevent abuse or misuse of the service;
  • respond to support requests.

We do not use Smart Money Tracker to sell users’ personal information.

11. Data Retention

Local Watchlist and preference data remains on your device until you remove it, clear applicable app data, or delete the App.

Subscription-related information may be retained by Apple and Adapty for as long as necessary to process purchases, maintain subscription status, restore purchases, comply with applicable legal obligations, and provide subscription-related services.

Information submitted directly to us through a support request may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain support records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or satisfy legal obligations.

We do not retain personal information longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

12. Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

Because Smart Money Tracker does not currently require a user account, much of the App’s user-specific information is stored locally or associated with anonymous service identifiers.

You may remove locally stored App information by deleting relevant Watchlist items or deleting the App.

If you would like to request access to or deletion of personal information that may be under our control, contact support@smartmoneytracker.app.

We may need limited information to identify the relevant record or subscription profile. Certain purchase and transaction records are controlled by Apple and may be subject to Apple’s own retention requirements and privacy procedures.

13. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through Smart Money Tracker.

However, no internet transmission, software system, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Children’s Privacy

Smart Money Tracker is not directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

15. International Processing

Third-party service providers used by Smart Money Tracker may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Such providers are responsible for handling information according to their applicable privacy obligations and policies.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Smart Money Tracker, our service providers, or applicable requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page and the “Last updated” date will be revised.
